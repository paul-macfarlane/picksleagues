## Intent

The dashboard's "New season available" pill (`LeagueCard` in
`apps/web/src/routes/_authed/index.tsx`) renders identically for every member,
but only a commissioner can act on it (`RENEW_SEASON` is commissioner-only).
Split the render by role: a commissioner keeps the actionable opportunity pill;
every other member sees that the new season is waiting on a commissioner. No
control moves — the renew action stays on the league overview, where the card
already links (ADR-0009). No API, schema, or contract change.

## Facts the plan rests on (surveyed 2026-08-06)

- `LeagueSummary` (`packages/schemas/src/leagues.ts`) already carries both
  `myRole` and `renewable` — the card already reads `myRole` for its
  Commissioner pill, so the split needs nothing new on the wire.
- `renewable` is **derived on read, never stored** (`isRenewable` in
  `apps/api/src/services/leagues/current-season.ts`: latest ingested
  `sport_seasons.year` for the mode's sport vs the league's current-instance
  year). Inserting a newer season row flips it on the next read — no job run
  needed for fixtures.
- `RENEW_SEASON` is commissioner-only and window-free ("anytime") in the action
  matrix (`packages/schemas/src/league-actions.ts`); the league overview
  (`routes/_authed/leagues/$leagueId/index.tsx`) already gates its renew
  control with `canActOnLeague(..., LEAGUE_ACTION.RENEW_SEASON, now)`.
- `StatusPill` (`apps/web/src/components/status-pill.tsx`) tones are semantic
  (`neutral`, `accent`, `highlight`, …) and a tone never carries meaning alone —
  the pill's word does.
- The server-side `renewable` signal is already pinned by integration tests,
  including on the my-leagues list (`apps/api/test/renewal.test.ts`,
  "renewable signal on reads").
- No e2e or unit test binds to the pill today (`grep` across `e2e/` and
  `apps/web`): nothing to migrate, nothing that a copy change would break.

## Decisions resolved into the plan

1. **The role answers "whose it is" — no named commissioners.** The
   non-commissioner variant says the season is waiting on *a commissioner*,
   not on a named person: `LeagueSummary` carries no member identities (adding
   them is a contract change plus payload for a hint); a league may have
   several commissioners with identical powers (spec §Commissioner Powers), so
   naming one is wrong and naming all doesn't fit a pill; and rendering a
   person would owe the `UserIdentity` component, which is card-body weight,
   not pill weight. The card links into the league, where members and roles
   are listed. If the owner wants named commissioners instead, that is a
   contract change — escalate before implementing.
2. **Proposed copy — presentation policy, owner may reword freely.**
   Commissioner: `New season available` (unchanged). Everyone else:
   `New season — waiting on a commissioner`. Nothing binds to either string.
3. **Tone split mirrors actionability.** Commissioner keeps `highlight` (an
   opportunity they can act on); the member variant uses `neutral` (status
   about someone else's action). The wording, not the tone, carries the
   distinction, per the `StatusPill` doc comment.
4. **No new automated tests.** Which pill a role sees is presentation policy
   (engineering rules: don't unit-test presentation policy; e2e covers
   journeys, not branches), and the domain rules underneath — who may renew,
   when `renewable` is true — are already pinned server-side in
   `apps/api/test/renewal.test.ts`. Evidence is visual, per the map below.
